# About Me

I am studying for an MSc in Artificial Intelligence with the University of Essex Online. My academic background combines psychology, blockchain technologies and artificial intelligence, giving me an interdisciplinary interest in both computational systems and the human contexts in which they operate.

My professional experience includes work across e-commerce, digital media, platform management and workflow automation. I have also explored practical uses of artificial intelligence through API-based applications, automation platforms and AI-assisted content workflows.

## Academic background

- BSc in Psychology
- MSc in Blockchain Technologies
- MSc Artificial Intelligence — University of Essex Online, in progress

## Areas of interest

- Intelligent agents and autonomous systems
- Explainable and responsible artificial intelligence
- Automation and AI-assisted workflows
- Human interaction with algorithmic systems
- Data-informed business and financial decision-making
- Privacy, fairness, trust and governance in AI

## Professional direction

My goal is to strengthen the technical and analytical skills required to design useful AI systems while retaining a critical understanding of their social, ethical and organisational consequences. This portfolio records that development through evidence rather than unsupported claims.

## Portfolio approach

Each module section includes relevant artefacts, feedback, reflective analysis and a mapping between the evidence and the module learning outcomes. Personal or commercially sensitive information is excluded from the public portfolio.
