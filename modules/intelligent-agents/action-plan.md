# Professional Development Plan

The action plan will be derived from actual weaknesses identified in the skills matrix, feedback and reflection. Vague goals such as “improve coding” will be replaced by specific actions and evidence of success.

| Development need | Specific action | Evidence of success | Target date | Progress |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| To be identified | To be defined | To be defined | To be set | Not started |

## SMART test

Every action should be:

- **Specific** about the behaviour or skill;
- **Measurable** through evidence;
- **Achievable** within the available time and resources;
- **Relevant** to the module or professional direction;
- **Time-bound** by a clear review date.
