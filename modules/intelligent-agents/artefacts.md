## Description template

For every substantial artefact, record:

- **Purpose:** What problem or requirement did it address?
- **Description:** What was produced?
- **My contribution:** What did I personally do?
- **Tools and techniques:** What was used and why?
- **Result:** What worked?
- **Limitations:** What did not work or remain unresolved?
- **Feedback:** What was received?
- **Action taken:** What changed afterward?
- **Learning outcomes:** Which outcomes does the evidence support?
