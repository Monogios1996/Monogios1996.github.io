# Unit 6 — Individual Peer Evaluation

**Activity:** Individual peer evaluation of the Group E team design project  
**Submission date:** 7 September 2026  
**Status:** Submitted

## Evidence and purpose

I completed an individual peer-evaluation form for Group E, assessing teammates against the criteria specified in the exercise: attendance and punctuality, contributions to discussions, completing assigned work on time, quality of deliverables, cooperation and support, and contribution to project success.

I supplied written justifications as well as ratings. These focused on observable working behaviours, such as meeting participation, organisational leadership, constructive discussion, completion of assigned tasks and collaboration through ongoing team communication.

## Professional reflection

This exercise asked me to consider teamwork as more than whether the final design document was completed. Different team members contributed through meetings, leadership, research, drafting and asynchronous communication. The evaluation also required distinguishing punctuality and meeting attendance from overall contribution and quality of work.

**Privacy note:** The submitted individual form includes named classmates, individual ratings and qualitative judgements. Those personal evaluations are not reproduced in this public e-Portfolio. The original form is retained as a private source record.
