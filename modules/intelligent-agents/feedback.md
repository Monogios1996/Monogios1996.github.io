## Feedback workflow

**Feedback → interpretation → revision → result**

The discussion activities demonstrate this cycle clearly. Peer feedback did not simply confirm my original arguments; it identified underdeveloped concepts, challenged assumptions and supplied alternative perspectives. I used those points to refine the later summary posts and make the final positions more precise.

Verbal feedback will be labelled as a dated personal record rather than presented as a direct quotation unless the wording was formally provided in writing.
