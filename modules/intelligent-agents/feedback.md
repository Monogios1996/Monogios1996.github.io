## Feedback workflow

**Feedback → interpretation → revision → result**

The first discussion demonstrates this cycle clearly. The peer feedback did not simply confirm my original argument; it identified concepts and mechanisms that were underdeveloped. I used those points to refine the Unit 3 summary and make the final position more precise.

Verbal feedback will be labelled as a dated personal record rather than presented as a direct quotation unless the wording was formally provided in writing.
