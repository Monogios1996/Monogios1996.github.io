# Reflective Piece

The final reflective piece will be written near the end of the module and will cover all four learning outcomes. The exact module word limit and submission instructions must be checked before the final draft is completed.

## Planned framework: What? So what? What next?

### What?

A concise account of selected experiences, including discussions, technical work, team activity, feedback and important difficulties.

### So what?

Critical analysis of decisions, assumptions, alternatives, artefacts, feedback, prior experience and relevant literature. This will form the largest section.

### What next?

Specific changes to technical practice, teamwork, professional development and future study, linked directly to the action plan.

## Evidence prompts

- Which design choice was most important, and why?
- Which assumption proved weak or incorrect?
- What does an artefact demonstrate, and what does it fail to demonstrate?
- How did feedback alter the work?
- Which alternative perspective was stronger than my initial view?
- What would I do differently under the same constraints?
- How will this learning transfer to professional practice?
