## Unit 6 — Group E Design Proposal

**Team output:** Design proposal for an LLM-powered academic research and information-gathering planning agent, including requirements, six agent roles, technology choices, design rationale, anticipated risks and two diagrams.

**My contribution:** I was mainly responsible for creating and refining the system overview and UML sequence diagram and checking consistency between both diagrams and the written proposal. My review covered component naming, the success and failure routes, bounded retries and replanning, the distinction between agent roles and supporting tools, and human approval before final export.

**Design methods:** The team proposed a layered hybrid architecture, adapted Prometheus modelling, LangGraph orchestration and a bounded Plan-and-Execute workflow. The original group submission was a design document rather than a working implementation.

**Professional development:** Coordinating diagrams with shared text demonstrated the importance of clear ownership, consistent terminology and inspection of control paths in a team design.

**Evidence:** [View detailed project record]({{ '/modules/intelligent-agents/evidence/artefacts/unit-06-group-e-team-design/' | relative_url }})
