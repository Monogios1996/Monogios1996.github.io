# Machine Learning — Learning outcomes

[Module overview]({{ '/modules/machine-learning/' | relative_url }})

This mapping uses the **two outcomes stated in the supplied Unit 3 activity brief**. It does not assign invented module-wide outcome numbers or claim to reproduce the full module specification.

| Stated activity outcome | Relevant evidence | What it demonstrates | Limit of evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Articulate the legal, social, ethical and professional issues faced by machine learning professionals. | ML-01: professional implications, attribution, dataset provenance and limitations | Explains responsible treatment of outliers, appropriate data use, privacy considerations and the need to communicate uncertainty and avoid unsupported real-world claims | Analytical discussion of responsibilities; not a compliance audit or deployed-system assessment |
