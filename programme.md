## Evidence standard

For every module, the portfolio will aim to show:

- what was produced;
- why particular methods or decisions were used;
- my individual contribution;
- feedback received and action taken;
- strengths, limitations and lessons learned;
- explicit links to module learning outcomes;
- future development through a professional action plan.
